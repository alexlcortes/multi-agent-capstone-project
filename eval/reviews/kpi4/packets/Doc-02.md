## 1. Executive summary
Target: Residential price/value switchers currently on a competitor in Ocala, FL / Marion County, FL.
Value proposition: Local Ocala fiber: multi-gig symmetric speeds, included Wi‑Fi and local technicians — promotional pricing built for value switchers. [SWOT-S1, EV-33d161e7, THEME-1, THEME-2]
Positioning: Wire3 positions as Ocala's local fiber alternative to incumbents: predictable introductory value, superior reliability from local technicians, and included home Wi‑Fi for multi-gig households. [THEME-1, THEME-2, SWOT-S1, SWOT-W1]
The plan recommends 5 channels (Local search ads + promo landing pages (PPC), Local SEO, city build pages & content, Direct mail & neighborhood technician outreach, Mobile carrier partnerships & co-marketing, Customer referral & local influencer programs), 3 launch phases and 4 success metrics, and lists 3 risks and 3 follow-up research questions.

## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Promotions and short-term price offers drive trial signups
- Description: Providers use introductory monthly prices, free trial months, gift cards, or streaming credits to attract price-sensitive households; promos are a primary trigger for new signups in Ocala.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- Evidence: EV-ca45782e, EV-f536aad1, EV-0cc2c883, EV-34d1637a

### THEME-2: Reliability & local support motivate switches away from incumbents
- Description: Claims of faster, more consistent speeds and local U.S.-based technicians (and faster onsite response) are cited as reasons households switch from legacy providers.
- Related research questions: RQ4, RQ1
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum
- Evidence: EV-297cf0f1, EV-764b1863, EV-113bb33f, EV-72f1851e

### THEME-3: Mobile + broadband bundles are a meaningful value lever
- Description: Mobile plan discounts or included/discounted mobile lines (Spectrum Mobile, T-Mobile discounts, AT&T mix-and-match) are promoted as bill-savings and influence value-seeking switchers.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ6
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-fa4ca232, EV-aef41e78, EV-3ccb3abd, EV-066204c1

### THEME-4: Fixed wireless is positioned as a low-cost alternative but has variability
- Description: Fixed wireless (T-Mobile Home Internet) is promoted as lower-cost and easy to install; third-party and user reports flag variable speeds and prioritization policies that matter for heavy users.
- Related research questions: RQ2, RQ4
- Competitors involved: T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-9c3ea513, EV-3605d691, EV-9b796c59, EV-0cc2c883

## 7. SWOT analysis

### Strengths
- SWOT-S1: Wire3 is rapidly expanding 100% fiber networks in multiple Florida communities and advertises multi‑gig symmetric tiers. [EV-9711e8d0, EV-d7118dd5, EV-bab0d975]
- SWOT-S2: Spectrum has a large incumbent footprint in Ocala with bundled services (TV, mobile) and multiple speed tiers. [EV-f536aad1, EV-4c6c24d8]

### Weaknesses
- SWOT-W1: Wire3 is a regional brand with lower national recognition; consumer curiosity exists but awareness is limited. [EV-4b556634, EV-33d161e7]
- SWOT-W2: Promotional price cliffs (post-promo uncertainty) create bill‑shock risk for customers of incumbents. [EV-f536aad1, EV-58fa6c25, EV-3d871838]

### Opportunities
- SWOT-O1: Affordable connectivity programs and county broadband initiatives increase demand among budget-conscious households. [EV-42545655, EV-3b544b50]
- SWOT-O2: Mobile + broadband bundling is a practical lever to capture value-seeking switchers. [EV-fa4ca232, EV-aef41e78]

### Threats
- SWOT-T1: Large incumbents (Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile) offer aggressive promos, wide coverage, and integrated mobile bundles that raise competitive barriers. [EV-f536aad1, EV-3ccb3abd, EV-aef41e78]
- SWOT-T2: Fixed wireless speed variability and heavy-user deprioritization policies can undermine customer satisfaction for wireless-first providers. [EV-3605d691, EV-9c3ea513]

## 15. Launch phases and activities

- Timeframe: Weeks 0-4 pre-launch
- Activities:

### PHASE-2: Acquisition push (promos + local conversion)
- Sequence order: 2
- Timeframe: Launch month (Month 1)
- Activities: 
  - Run targeted search PPC and social promos with one-month-free offers and clear post-promo pricing on landing pages. [THEME-1, EV-ca45782e, SWOT-W2]
  - Deploy neighborhood technician outreach, direct mail and local events demonstrating white-glove installs and included Wi‑Fi. [EV-33d161e7, THEME-2, SWOT-W1]
  - Activate mobile carrier partner credits for qualifying signups (co-marketing) to mimic bundle value. [THEME-3, ASM-1, SWOT-O2]
- Exit criteria: Acquisition targets met (new subscribers in month 1 >= success metric target) and promo conversion tracked; partner credits live.

### PHASE-3: Retention & referral scaling
- Sequence order: 3
- Timeframe: Months 2-6 post-launch
- Activities: 
  - Onboard customers with white-glove setup, ensure speed tests and local support satisfaction, and solicit referrals with credit incentives. [EV-33d161e7, THEME-2, THEME-1]
  - Monitor and publish transparent post-promo pricing and blended 12-month effective pricing to reduce churn from bill shock. [SWOT-W2, THEME-1]
- Exit criteria: Churn and NPS within target thresholds; referral program drives a measurable % of new customer acquisitions.
