## 1. Executive summary
Target: Residential price/value switchers currently on a competitor in Ocala, FL / Marion County, FL.
Value proposition: Local 100% fiber that delivers predictable bills, white‑glove installs, and real value without bundle complexity. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1]
Positioning: Wire3 is the regional fiber alternative to national incumbents: simple, local, and reliable internet with predictable pricing and superior installation experience. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1]
The plan recommends 5 channels (Search + geo-targeted paid social, Local SEO & availability landing pages, Neighborhood direct mail & door-hanger campaigns, Community & employer partnerships (incl. ACP outreach), Customer referral credits & installer-led signups), 3 launch phases and 3 success metrics, and lists 3 risks and 3 follow-up research questions.

## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Bundles & bill shock drive switching
- Description: Customers respond strongly to bundled offers (internet + mobile + TV) and often switch because promo cliffs or combined-bill perceived savings change post-promo — bundling is used as a retention and acquisition lever by incumbents.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ5
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-84e82536, EV-aef41e78, EV-83e823a3, EV-b5f4297d

### THEME-2: Price guarantees and long-run certainty matter for budget households
- Description: Five-year price locks or guarantees (publicized by some fixed-wireless providers) and transparent 12-month promo framing reduce churn risk for price-sensitive households.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2
- Competitors involved: T-Mobile Home Internet, Spectrum
- Evidence: EV-ead5147a, EV-944ad5f5, EV-f536aad1

### THEME-3: Regional fiber entrants emphasize transparent pricing and local support
- Description: New regional fiber ISPs position on simplified bills, local technicians, free or included Wi‑Fi devices and white‑glove installs to differentiate against large incumbents with complex promos.
- Related research questions: RQ7
- Competitors involved: Wire3
- Evidence: EV-31d15ec1, EV-267cec38, EV-cc457b54, EV-fe02126f

### THEME-4: Reliability and installation experience influence churn more than headline speed
- Description: Beyond headline speeds, customers cite reliability, installation experience and billing surprises as the most frequent triggers for switching providers.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ4
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, Wire3
- Evidence: EV-36744013, EV-72f1851e, EV-764b1863

## 7. SWOT analysis

### Strengths
- SWOT-S1: Wire3 advertises symmetrical, multi-gig fiber (up to 10 Gbps) and emphasizes simple, transparent pricing. [EV-120af567, EV-31d15ec1]
- SWOT-S2: Spectrum has wide existing footprint in Ocala with bundle options (Internet, TV, Mobile) and promotional pricing. [EV-157b1b2e, EV-f536aad1]

### Weaknesses
- SWOT-W1: Wire3 faces early installation complaints in some communities (reports of lawn damage, cut lines) that could slow adoption. [EV-72f1851e]
- SWOT-W2: Spectrum customers report bill increases after promo periods ('promo cliff' / bill shock). [EV-929f555a, EV-a11de057]

### Opportunities
- SWOT-O1: High local demand for affordable student/home internet (programs like ACP) and households seeking stable bills open a market for simple fixed-price fiber offers. [EV-42545655, EV-9a4e606f]
- SWOT-O2: Incumbent promotional complexity and rising cost-of-living create openings for regional providers offering transparent pricing and local service. [EV-83e823a3, EV-cc457b54]

### Threats
- SWOT-T1: National carriers (T-Mobile, AT&T) use mobile+internet bundling, price guarantees, and promotions that can blunt regional fiber customer acquisition. [EV-aef41e78, EV-3ccb3abd, EV-ee1b74c0]
- SWOT-T2: Operational issues during rapid buildout (installation quality, community friction) could damage brand trust for new entrants. [EV-72f1851e, EV-70f181f8]

## 15. Launch phases and activities

### PHASE-1: Local awareness & availability
- Sequence order: 1
- Timeframe: 0-2 months (initial market window)
- Activities: 
  - Run geo-targeted search and social campaigns tied to availability checks; publish clear landing pages showing pricing and install promises. [inference; EV-cc457b54, THEME-3]
  - Engage community partners and ACP outreach channels to seed low-income household awareness. [SWOT-O1, THEME-2]
- Exit criteria: Availability-check conversion lift (baseline X→Y) and initial awareness metrics met (e.g., availability page visits threshold).

### PHASE-2: Acquisition & onboarding
- Sequence order: 2
- Timeframe: 2-6 months
- Activities: 
  - Offer targeted short promos (e.g., first month free / streaming credits) with clear post-promo MSRP and price-certainty messaging. [EV-cc457b54, EV-fe02126f, THEME-2]
  - Deliver free white-glove basic setup and ensure installer QA to reduce early negative install experiences. [EV-31d15ec1, SWOT-W1]
  - Enable installer-led signups and immediate referral enrollment at install time. [inference; THEME-3, EV-31d15ec1]
- Exit criteria: Target number of installs achieved with post-install NPS above threshold and conversion rate from availability checks hitting target.

### PHASE-3: Retention, proof & scale
- Sequence order: 3
- Timeframe: 6-12 months
- Activities: 
  - Pilot a price-stability or limited guarantee option for early cohorts to reduce churn risk and collect evidence against competitor guarantees. [inference; THEME-2, EV-a89f66b2]
  - Scale referral program and deepen employer/community partnerships to sustain low-cost acquisition. [inference; SWOT-O1, THEME-3]
  - Monitor install QA, customer support SLAs, and local sentiment; remediate operational issues quickly. [SWOT-T2, EV-31d15ec1]
- Exit criteria: Churn below target, referral-driven installs contribute X% of new adds, and unit economics trending to plan.
