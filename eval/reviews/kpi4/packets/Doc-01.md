## 1. Executive summary
Target: Residential price/value switchers currently on a competitor in Ocala, FL / Marion County, FL.
Value proposition: Local 100% FTTH delivering symmetric multi-gig performance with transparent pricing and local support. [SWOT-S1, THEME-2]
Positioning: Wire3 is the local fiber-first provider focused on delivering true symmetric multi-gig performance, straightforward pricing, and faster local support—positioned as the high-performance alternative to bundle-driven incumbents. [SWOT-S1, THEME-2, SWOT-T1]
The plan recommends 5 channels (Geo-targeted social & search ads, Local SEO & availability landing pages, Community events & local sponsorships, Partnerships (MVNOs / streaming services), Customer referral program), 3 launch phases and 3 success metrics, and lists 3 risks and 2 follow-up research questions.

## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Promotions and short-term price reductions
- Description: Multiple providers (Wire 3, T‑Mobile, Spectrum) are using time-limited promotions (first-month free, streaming credits, reduced monthly for 12 months) to attract new customers and accelerate take rates during buildouts.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
- Competitors involved: Wire3, T-Mobile Home Internet, Spectrum
- Evidence: EV-ca45782e, EV-0cc2c883, EV-f536aad1

### THEME-2: Regional fiber expansion and community builds
- Description: Smaller regional fiber operators (Wire 3) are actively building 100% fiber networks in towns and counties (e.g., Titusville, Palm Bay, Jacksonville), creating local competition to incumbent cable operators.
- Related research questions: RQ4, RQ5, RQ6
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-b6f8677c, EV-bab0d975, EV-37184440

### THEME-3: Fixed‑wireless (5G) as a competitive low-friction alternative
- Description: T‑Mobile and other 5G/home-wireless offerings advertise simple self-install, price-locks, and no annual contracts, positioning fixed-wireless as a quick competitor in areas lacking fiber.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ3
- Competitors involved: T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-a79f651f, EV-9c3ea513

## 7. SWOT analysis

### Strengths
- SWOT-S1: Wire 3 offers 100% FTTH with plans up to 10 Gbps and emphasizes local customer support and transparent pricing. [EV-120af567, EV-d7118dd5, EV-267cec38]

### Weaknesses
- SWOT-W1: As a regional buildout, Wire 3’s availability is street-by-street and limited to specific counties; rollout speed may lag incumbents in some neighborhoods. [EV-d94963c8, EV-01b0d2c0]

### Opportunities
- SWOT-O1: Promotional offers (free month, streaming credits) can accelerate customer acquisition during new-city launches. [EV-ca45782e, EV-fc020f49]

### Threats
- SWOT-T1: Large incumbents (Spectrum, AT&T, T‑Mobile) offer bundled services, national marketing and rapid promotions which may pressure regional pricing and retention. [EV-7803b81d, EV-5159406a, EV-a79f651f]

## 8. 7P market analysis

### Product
- Market description: The competitive market includes full-fiber regional entrants (Wire 3), national cable operators (Spectrum) offering fiber-powered HFC, national telco fiber (AT&T), and 5G fixed-wireless (T‑Mobile). Each positions different tech attributes (symmetry, latency, installation model) to target distinct customer needs. [EV-120af567, EV-7803b81d, EV-a79f651f, EV-6016b158]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3’s product stack includes residential tiers from 250 Mbps up to 10 Gbps, free eero Wi‑Fi devices and optional managed services (Total Connect). [EV-d7118dd5, EV-33d161e7]

### Price
- Market description: Pricing strategies in the market range from low-cost fixed-wireless starting mid-$30s (with bundling discounts) to promotional 12-month pricing from incumbents; equipment fees and post-promo price changes are common. [EV-a79f651f, EV-0cc2c883, EV-f536aad1]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 ran a limited-time summer offer with one month free and reduced monthly pricing across residential tiers (e.g., Liftoff 250 Mbps at $30/mo promo). [EV-ca45782e, EV-89785427]

### Place
- Market description: Distribution varies: incumbents use broad-national footprints and retail + install teams; fixed-wireless is widely available quickly; regional fiber requires street-by-street construction and address eligibility checks. [EV-7803b81d, EV-d94963c8]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 is actively expanding in Central Florida and nearby states with targeted city buildouts (Ocala, Palm Bay, Jacksonville, Anderson SC) and address-based availability checks on its site. [EV-d94963c8, EV-bab0d975, EV-37184440]

### Promotion
- Market description: Providers frequently use short-term promotions (free months, streaming credits, gift cards) plus bundling discounts (mobile + internet) to lower effective first-year costs. [EV-0cc2c883, EV-ca45782e, EV-f536aad1]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3’s summer campaign included price reductions across plans, one month free for new customers, and complimentary streaming offers on select tiers. [EV-ca45782e, EV-fe02126f]

### People
- Market description: Customer experience emphasis varies: regional fiber providers market local technicians and US-based support; incumbents point to scale and bundled customer care across services. [EV-267cec38, EV-7803b81d, EV-3e13c51c]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 highlights local Florida/Georgia-based support teams, fast response times and professional installation included in many plans. [EV-267cec38, EV-3e13c51c]

### Process
- Market description: Installation models include self-install for fixed-wireless (fast rollout) and professional drop/installation for fiber; promotional onboarding often includes equipment and installation credits. [EV-33d161e7, EV-f536aad1]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 offers professional installation (eero device included) and markets simplified, transparent billing processes without hidden fees. [EV-33d161e7, EV-d19202dc]

### Physical evidence
- Market description: Physical network presence (fiber trenches, headends, wireless gateways) and retail/support footprints influence perceived availability and reliability. [EV-bab0d975, EV-37184440]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 has announced multiple funded construction projects (e.g., Palm Bay $100M, Titusville $11.5M), and launched city network rollouts indicating tangible build progress. [EV-bab0d975, EV-b6f8677c]

## 10. Customer pains and desired outcomes

### PAIN-1
- Pain statement: Promotional pricing creates initial uptake but leads to confusion and potential churn when post-promo rates apply. [THEME-1, ASM-1]
- Desired outcome: Clear, long-term pricing guarantees or transparent post-promo communication to reduce surprise churn. [inference]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-3
- Severity: high [SWOT-T1]

### PAIN-2
- Pain statement: Street-by-street fiber availability means many interested customers are ineligible during rollouts and default to incumbents. [SWOT-W1, THEME-2]
- Desired outcome: Faster eligibility notifications, transparent waitlists, and staged installation commitments to capture intent until service is available. [inference]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-2
- Severity: high [SWOT-W1]

### PAIN-3
- Pain statement: Customers seeking bundled mobile + internet discounts may select incumbents offering integrated bundles. [SWOT-T1, THEME-1]
- Desired outcome: Partnerships or targeted promotions that bridge the bundle gap (e.g., MVNO credits, streaming credits) to compete on effective first-year cost. [inference]
- Related icp ids: ICP-3, ICP-1
- Severity: medium [SWOT-T1]

## 15. Launch phases and activities

### PHASE-1: Pre-launch & community activation
- Sequence order: 1
- Timeframe: 1-3 months before first service availability
- Activities:
- Build and promote address-based waitlist and targeted landing pages; run pre-launch sign-up promos (first-month free/streaming credit). [THEME-2, THEME-1]
- Engage local stakeholders and run community events to raise awareness. [THEME-2]
- Exit criteria: Minimum waitlist of targeted households reached and promotional creative approved for launch.

### PHASE-2: Launch & initial conversions
- Sequence order: 2
- Timeframe: 0-3 months from first installs
- Activities:
- Execute geo-targeted paid ads, activate referral bonuses, and convert waitlist into installations with limited-time promotional pricing. [THEME-1, SWOT-O1]
- Ensure professional installation capacity and local tech support for first customers to build case studies and local testimonials. [SWOT-S1, THEME-2]
- Exit criteria: Achieve target initial-conversion rate and positive first-month NPS from early customers.

### PHASE-3: Scale & retention
- Sequence order: 3
- Timeframe: Ongoing after initial city launch
- Activities:
- Introduce partnerships (MVNO/streaming) to close bundle gaps, scale paid acquisition efficiently, and optimize pricing cadence to maintain ARPU. [SWOT-T1, THEME-1]
- Extend referral programs and local sponsorships to sustain low-cost growth and community trust. [THEME-2]
- Exit criteria: Sustained month-over-month net additions and improved churn relative to pre-launch cohort.

## 17. Risks

### RISK-1
- Statement: Post-promo price increases or unclear post-promo rates cause elevated churn after promotional periods. [THEME-1, ASM-1, SWOT-T1]
- Likelihood: medium [THEME-1]
- Impact: high [SWOT-T1]
- Mitigation: Publish clear post-promo pricing up front, offer optional price-locks or loyalty credits, and coordinate retention offers before promo expiry. [inference; THEME-1]

### RISK-2
- Statement: Construction delays and street-by-street eligibility constraints limit addressable market and slow growth. [SWOT-W1, THEME-2]
- Likelihood: medium [SWOT-W1]
- Impact: high [THEME-2]
- Mitigation: Implement transparent waitlists, staged rollout communications, and prioritize high-conversion streets to maximize early ARPU. [inference; THEME-2]

### RISK-3
- Statement: Incumbent bundle and large-scale promotional capacity may undercut Wire3 on effective first-year price. [SWOT-T1]
- Likelihood: high [SWOT-T1]
- Impact: medium [SWOT-T1]
- Mitigation: Develop targeted partnership offers and short-term promotional credits to match incumbents' effective economics while highlighting superior performance and local support. [inference; THEME-1, SWOT-T1]
