## 1. Executive summary
Target: Residential price/value switchers currently on a competitor in Ocala, FL / Marion County, FL.
Value proposition: Symmetric multi‑gig fiber with transparent pricing and local white‑glove support that reduces total cost of ownership versus bundled incumbents. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1, THEME-2]
Positioning: Wire3 is the local regional fiber alternative to national incumbents—offering symmetric multi‑gig performance, transparent pricing and faster local support rather than relying on long-term bundle lock-in. [THEME-3, THEME-1, THEME-2, SWOT-S1]
The plan recommends 5 channels (Targeted paid search & social (promo + TCO messaging), Local SEO & availability pages, Community partnerships & ACP outreach, Local events, signage & direct outreach, Referral program (customer credits)), 3 launch phases and 4 success metrics, and lists 3 risks and 4 follow-up research questions.

## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Bundled mobile offers shift value-sensitive switchers
- Description: Mobile discounts and included lines (Spectrum Mobile, T-Mobile bundle discounts, AT&T wireless offers) are commonly used by incumbents to lower effective broadband net cost and influence switch decisions.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-fa4ca232, EV-aef41e78, EV-3ccb3abd

### THEME-2: Promotional pricing creates a first-year 'cliff' risk
- Description: Major providers use 12-month promo pricing (Spectrum) or short promotional credits (T-Mobile) that reduce the first-year effective price but cause bill shock at renewal.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- Evidence: EV-f536aad1, EV-0cc2c883, EV-3d871838

### THEME-3: Regional fiber entrants emphasize performance + local support
- Description: New regional 100% fiber ISPs (Wire 3) promote symmetric multi-gig speeds, transparent pricing, local technicians and targeted promotions to win customers from national incumbents.
- Related research questions: RQ4, RQ5, RQ7
- Competitors involved: Wire3
- Evidence: EV-140af88d, EV-9711e8d0, EV-cc457b54

### THEME-4: Reliability and real-world speed / install realities drive churn
- Description: Customers cite reliability and install/activation experience as drivers to switch: comparisons between cable/HFC and 5G FWA highlight perceived tradeoffs in consistency versus price.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ4
- Competitors involved: Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- Evidence: EV-764b1863, EV-9c3ea513, EV-ec67432b

## 7. SWOT analysis

### Strengths
- SWOT-S1: Wire3 advertises symmetrical fiber speeds up to 10 Gbps and positions itself as 100% fiber. [EV-140af88d]
- SWOT-S2: Spectrum has broad availability in Ocala and well-known bundle options (internet, TV, mobile). [EV-7803b81d, EV-4c6c24d8]
- SWOT-S3: T-Mobile and AT&T offer mobile-bundle discounts that reduce effective monthly cost for many households. [EV-aef41e78, EV-3ccb3abd]

### Weaknesses
- SWOT-W1: Wire3 is still building out coverage (limited footprint compared with incumbents), creating a smaller addressable base today. [EV-9711e8d0, EV-bab0d975]
- SWOT-W2: Community reports exist of installation issues and construction complaints for Wire3 in some localities. [EV-72f1851e, EV-70f181f8]
- SWOT-W3: Spectrum uses promotional first-year pricing which leads to post-promo bill increases (churn risk due to perceived price hikes). [EV-f536aad1, EV-929f555a]

### Opportunities
- SWOT-O1: Target price-sensitive households that are upset by incumbent rate hikes (e.g., Reddit complaints about Spectrum price increases). [EV-a11de057]
- SWOT-O2: Promotional offers (first month free, streaming months) can lower acquisition friction for a regional ISP. [EV-cc457b54, EV-fe02126f]
- SWOT-O3: Affordable Connectivity Program and device discounts are material to low-income households in Marion County. [EV-42545655]

### Threats
- SWOT-T1: Large incumbents (Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile) can bundle mobile and TV to create value propositions hard for a small regional ISP to match. [EV-fa4ca232, EV-4c6c24d8, EV-3ccb3abd]
- SWOT-T2: Competitive pressure and analyst scrutiny (Charter) indicate margin and subscriber-pressure risk for cable incumbents. [EV-1ba840f5, EV-19a83dcf]
- SWOT-T3: Fixed wireless performance variability and outages reported by customers could reduce trust in FWA alternatives. [EV-9c3ea513, EV-46ad40b5]

## 8. 7P market analysis

### Product
- Market description: The Ocala market features HFC cable providers (Spectrum), national fiber and wireless options (AT&T, T-Mobile), and emergent regional 100% fiber builders (Wire3). Speeds and product forms vary from DSL/fixed wireless to multi-gig fiber. [EV-7803b81d, EV-6016b158, EV-9711e8d0]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 promotes 100% fiber with symmetric speeds up to 10 Gbps and local support. [EV-140af88d, EV-287cef5e]

### Price
- Market description: Incumbents commonly use promotional pricing (12-month promos or bill credits) and bundle discounts; advertised entry prices vary by tech and location. [EV-f536aad1, EV-a79f651f, EV-50593ed7]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 has used limited-time offers (first month free, streaming credits) to lower acquisition cost and advertise transparent pricing. [EV-cc457b54, EV-fe02126f]

### Place
- Market description: Distribution is address-by-address; incumbents have large footprints in Ocala, while regional fiber rollouts are staged city-by-city. [EV-157b1b2e, EV-9711e8d0]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 is actively building in Florida markets (Palm Bay, Jacksonville, Titusville) and signs local availability pages and press releases. [EV-bab0d975, EV-cc457b54]

### Promotion
- Market description: Promotions include first-year price guarantees, free months, streaming credits, and mobile-bundle discounts—used to capture new customers quickly. [EV-f536aad1, EV-0cc2c883, EV-cc457b54]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 runs seasonal promotions (e.g., summer offer) including a free month and streaming credits to increase signups. [EV-cc457b54, EV-fe02126f]

### People
- Market description: Customer experience and local technician responsiveness are meaningful differentiators in regional markets; many customers cite support interactions as reason to switch. [EV-287cef5e, EV-72f1851e]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 emphasizes local support teams and faster local technician response as a selling point. [EV-287cef5e, EV-31d15ec1]

### Process
- Market description: Install realities (self-install vs. technician drop/fiber splice) and one-time install charges materially affect customer decisions, especially in rural or multi-unit properties. [EV-ec67432b, EV-3d871838]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 advertises white-glove setup and simplified scheduling to reduce installation friction. [EV-31d15ec1]

### Physical evidence
- Market description: Physical network differences (HFC coaxial drops, fiber-to-home, fixed wireless antennas) determine performance characteristics customers experience. [EV-7803b81d, EV-140af88d, EV-87cea105]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire3 is investing in fiber buildouts (e.g., Palm Bay $100M project) and highlights the physical 100% fiber distinction. [EV-bab0d975, EV-140af88d]

## 9. Ideal customer profiles

### ICP-1: Price/value switchers (bill-shock sensitive households)

- Demographic signals: 
  - Households with multiple mobile lines that realize bundle discounts [THEME-1]
  - Recent complaints about incumbent rate increases or promotional cliff [SWOT-O1, THEME-2]

- Description: Households that prioritize consistent real-world speeds, low latency and smooth install/activation experiences over absolute lowest headline price. [THEME-4, THEME-3]
- Current provider: Spectrum, AT&T [EV-7803b81d, EV-6016b158]
- Demographic signals: 
  - Work-from-home or multi-device streaming households sensitive to outages and installation delays [THEME-4]

### ICP-3: Low-income / ACP-eligible households
- Description: Households for which government discounts (Affordable Connectivity Program) and device/installation assistance materially affect broadband affordability and switching behavior. [SWOT-O3]
- Current provider: T-Mobile Home Internet, Spectrum [EV-87cea105, EV-7803b81d]
- Demographic signals: 
  - Households likely to enroll in ACP and seek provider assistance to apply [SWOT-O3]

## 10. Customer pains and desired outcomes

### PAIN-1
- Pain statement: Bill shock from first-year promotional pricing that rises at renewal, making the true monthly cost unpredictable. [THEME-2, SWOT-W3]
- Desired outcome: Predictable, transparent total cost of service with no surprise post-promo spikes. [inference; THEME-2, SWOT-O2]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-3
- Severity: high [THEME-2]

### PAIN-2
- Pain statement: Incumbent mobile + internet bundle discounts reduce the effective broadband price, making it harder to justify switching to a non-bundled regional ISP. [THEME-1, SWOT-T1]
- Desired outcome: An acquisition value proposition that offsets bundle economics (clear TCO comparison, partner offers, ACP assistance). [inference; THEME-1, SWOT-O3]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-3
- Severity: high [THEME-1, SWOT-T1]

### PAIN-3
- Pain statement: Poor installation or inconsistent real-world speeds and support create churn and deter signups. [THEME-4, SWOT-W2]
- Desired outcome: Smooth white-glove or clearly scheduled installs and fast local technician response to ensure reliable experience from day one. [EV-31d15ec1, EV-287cef5e]
- Related icp ids: ICP-2, ICP-1
- Severity: medium [THEME-4]
