## 1. Executive summary
Target: Residential price/value switchers currently on a competitor in Ocala, FL / Marion County, FL.
Value proposition: Ocala's local 100% fiber: symmetric multi-gig speeds, professional installs, and straightforward pricing backed by local technicians. [SWOT-S1, THEME-4, EV-d7118dd5]
Positioning: Wire3 is the local-first fiber provider delivering symmetric multi-gig performance and community-based installation/support — positioned as the trustworthy, transparent alternative to national incumbents. [THEME-4, SWOT-S1]
The plan recommends 4 channels (Targeted digital paid (search & social), Local SEO & availability pages, Community events & direct outreach, Realtor / builder partnerships & referrals), 3 launch phases and 3 success metrics, and lists 2 risks and 2 follow-up research questions.

## 2. Research scope and evidence
20 planned research calls returned 472 evidence records retrieved 2026-09-23. Research questions:

- RQ8: What marketing channels are effective and cost-appropriate for a low-brand-recognition regional ISP in a mid-size FL market?

## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Aggressive short-term promotional pricing
- Description: Multiple providers are using limited-time promotions (free months, reduced introductory rates, streaming bundles) to drive sign-ups during summer 2026.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- Evidence: EV-ca45782e, EV-89785427, EV-0cc2c883

### THEME-2: Regional fiber buildouts increasing competitive pressure
- Description: Wire 3 is actively expanding funded fiber builds in multiple Florida markets, while larger incumbents continue to deploy fiber and hybrid solutions.
- Related research questions: RQ4, RQ5
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum, AT&T
- Evidence: EV-bab0d975, EV-b6f8677c, EV-dc496881

### THEME-3: 5G fixed wireless as a lower-friction alternative
- Description: T-Mobile and other national carriers promote 5G fixed wireless (and fiber where available) as quick-to-deploy home/business broadband alternatives with self-install gateways and price-lock guarantees.
- Related research questions: RQ1, RQ2
- Competitors involved: T-Mobile Home Internet, AT&T, Spectrum
- Evidence: EV-a79f651f, EV-ead5147a, EV-56f977f0

### THEME-4: Local customer-service/installation quality is a differentiator
- Description: Providers that emphasize local technicians and transparent billing (Wire 3 messaging) use service quality and honesty as a competitive advantage versus national incumbents.
- Related research questions: RQ7, RQ8
- Competitors involved: Wire3, Spectrum
- Evidence: EV-267cec38, EV-73f11d65, EV-113bb33f

### Product
- Market description: The residential and small-business broadband market in Ocala/Marion County is a mix of legacy cable/HFC, expanding fiber-to-the-home entrants, and fixed-wireless offerings; customers value reliable speeds, transparent pricing, and prompt local support. [EV-dc496881, EV-59451b96, EV-a79f651f]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 sells 100% fiber plans (250 Mbps to 10 Gbps) with add‑ons (eero router, Total Connect) and business/residential tiers. [EV-d7118dd5, EV-33d161e7]

### Price
- Market description: Competitors use promotional pricing (first‑year discounts, free months, streaming bundles) to lower initial effective price; incumbents often increase to standard rates after promo periods. [EV-f536aad1, EV-89785427, EV-0cc2c883]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 offered a summer promotion (one month free and reduced monthly pricing across tiers) through July 4, 2026. [EV-ca45782e, EV-88785294]

### Place
- Market description: Physical availability is street-by-street for new fiber builds; fixed wireless and cable remain widely available in many Ocala neighborhoods. [EV-dc496881, EV-a55605e4]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 has active construction and funded network projects in Florida markets (e.g., Palm Bay, Titusville, Jacksonville) and specifically began expansion into Marion County/Ocala. [EV-bab0d975, EV-de496ba7, EV-da49655b]

### Promotion
- Market description: Promotions are common and time-limited; providers bundle streaming, gift cards, or free months to accelerate sign-ups. [EV-0cc2c883, EV-ca45782e, EV-89785427]
- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3's summer offer included one month free and complimentary streaming (YouTube TV on select tiers) and reduced monthly pricing across plans. [EV-ca45782e, EV-88785294]

### People

- Wire3 relevant fact: Wire 3 emphasizes local Florida & Georgia-based support, 'no hidden fees', and technician response from community-based teams. [EV-267cec38, EV-e3110146]

### Process

### Physical evidence

## 10. Customer pains and desired outcomes

### PAIN-1
- Pain statement: Promotional introductory rates obscure the standard post-promo monthly price, risking customer bill shock after the promotion ends. [THEME-1, ASM-1, UNK-1]
- Desired outcome: Clear, upfront disclosure of post-promo standard price and a simple price-lock or transition offer to avoid churn and complaints. [THEME-1, ASM-1]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-2
- Severity: high [THEME-1, SWOT-T1]

### PAIN-2
- Pain statement: Installation delays or inconsistent technician quality in some communities lead to negative local reviews and churn. [SWOT-W1, THEME-4]
- Desired outcome: Reliable, timely professional installation with local technicians and clear scheduling/communications. [THEME-4, EV-33d161e7]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-2
- Severity: medium [SWOT-W1]

### PAIN-3
- Pain statement: Some customers prefer lower-friction self-install fixed wireless options for immediate service activation. [THEME-3, EV-ead5147a]
- Desired outcome: Faster activation options (e.g., a self-install gateway or temporary fixed-wireless fallback) to capture customers who prioritize immediate connectivity. [inference; THEME-3]
- Related icp ids: ICP-1, ICP-3
- Severity: medium [THEME-3]

## 17. Risks

### RISK-1
- Statement: National competitors respond with deeper short-term promotions or bundling, eroding Wire3's promo effectiveness. [THEME-1, SWOT-T1]
- Likelihood: high [THEME-1, SWOT-T1]
- Impact: high [SWOT-T1]
- Mitigation: Use highly targeted local offers, emphasize service/installation differentiation, and limit promo exposure to launch geographies to protect margin. [inference; THEME-4]

### RISK-2
- Statement: Installation capacity or technician quality issues could damage local reputation and increase churn. [SWOT-W1, THEME-2]
- Likelihood: medium [SWOT-W1]
- Impact: high [SWOT-W1]
- Mitigation: Prioritize staffing, contractual local technician partners, clear scheduling windows, and customer credits for missed SLAs; monitor feedback closely. [inference; EV-33d161e7, THEME-4]
