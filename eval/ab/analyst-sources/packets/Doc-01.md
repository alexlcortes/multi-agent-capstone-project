## 7. SWOT analysis

### Strengths
- **SWOT-S1:** Wire3 advertises up to 10 Gbps tiers and whole-home fiber, enabling a technical differentiation vs legacy HFC providers. [EV-a459fdea, EV-d7118dd5]
- **SWOT-S2:** Spectrum has a large nationwide footprint and bundled product set (Internet, TV, Mobile) that supports cross-sell. [EV-6fcc7888, EV-33618605]

### Weaknesses
- **SWOT-W1:** Wire3 is a regional brand with limited local awareness compared with national incumbents. [EV-4b556634, EV-3f555350]
- **SWOT-W2:** Spectrum has experienced price increases on legacy tiers, which may drive price-sensitive churn. [EV-9f22976b]

### Opportunities
- **SWOT-O1:** Promotional windows and streaming-bundle incentives provide opportunities to capture trial customers seeking value. [EV-fe02126f, EV-89785427]
- **SWOT-O2:** Rural and unserved expansion by incumbents creates whitespace where a regional fiber provider can win by rapid local deployment. [EV-56997533, EV-da49655b]

### Threats
- **SWOT-T1:** Fixed-wireless and national fiber rollouts (T-Mobile, AT&T) and satellite entrants (Starlink referenced in industry commentary) increase competitive intensity. [EV-7b85fa9b, EV-843b849f]
- **SWOT-T2:** Local installation complaints and construction disruptions posted in community groups raise reputational risk during builds. [EV-72f1851e]
