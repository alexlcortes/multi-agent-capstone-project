## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Promo-driven customer acquisition (promo-cliff risk)
- **Description:** Aggressive short-term promotions (first-month free, reduced monthly rates, virtual card rebates) drive trial; the post-promo 'cliff' is a documented trigger for customer re-evaluation or churn.
- **Related research questions:** RQ2
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-4f63202e, EV-89785427, EV-89076a03, EV-31d73084

### THEME-2: Local fiber buildout vs. incumbent expansion
- **Description:** Regional fiber builds (Wire 3) and incumbent expansions (Spectrum, AT&T) are active in Marion County; physical availability and construction schedules determine where competition is direct.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, Spectrum, AT&T
- **Evidence:** EV-d94963c8, EV-56997533, EV-da49655b

### THEME-3: Mobile bundling shifts perceived value for switchers
- **Description:** Mobile + broadband bundling (Spectrum Mobile, AT&T wireless discounts, T‑Mobile credits) shifts effective household cost and can be decisive for price/value-oriented customers.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ3
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-07620654, EV-3ccb3abd, EV-f01b77e6

### THEME-4: Speed-to-availability vs. performance differentiation
- **Description:** Fixed wireless sells on rapid availability and low install friction; fiber sells on peak/multi‑gig and symmetric performance — customers trade off time-to-activate vs. top-end performance.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet, Wire3
- **Evidence:** EV-ab4afe18, EV-7bb4d4f6, EV-3a643017
