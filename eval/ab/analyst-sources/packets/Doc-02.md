## 6. Market themes

- **Description:** Providers use strong introductory promos (Spectrum, AT&T, Wire3, T-Mobile) that can create bill shock when promos lapse; customers cite promo lengths and post-promo cliffs as switching triggers.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet, Wire3
- **Evidence:** EV-31d73084, EV-9320822a, EV-4f63202e, EV-506321c1, EV-89785427

### THEME-2: Installation, local support and reliability as differentiators
- **Description:** Local field technicians, professional installation and 100% fiber builds are emphasized by regional ISPs (Wire3) to address incumbent pain points around outsourced support and reliability.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2, RQ7
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, Spectrum
- **Evidence:** EV-d94963c8, EV-beca7d7f, EV-fe02126f, EV-56997533

### THEME-3: Mobile bundles materially affect value messaging
- **Description:** Spectrum and T-Mobile explicitly bundle mobile with internet offers and advertise discounts; mobile bundles are used to lower effective consumer cost and increase stickiness.
- **Related research questions:** RQ2

### THEME-4: Fixed wireless & fiber coexistence in mid-size markets
