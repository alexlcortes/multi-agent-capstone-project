## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Promotions & first-year pricing shape switch decisions (promo-cliff risk)
- **Description:** Competitors use prominent promotional pricing for the first 12 months (Spectrum, AT&T) or digital credits (T-Mobile) and local entrants use limited-time promotional bundles (Wire3). This creates bill-shock risk when promos expire and is a frequent churn driver.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2, RQ3
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet, Wire3
- **Evidence:** EV-31d73084, EV-91207f04, EV-89076a03, EV-4f63202e, EV-019b25db

### THEME-2: Network footprint & multi-technology competition in Marion County
- **Description:** Ocala / Marion County sees overlapping investments: legacy cable (Spectrum) expanding gigabit reach, national fiber (AT&T) presence, 5G/fixed wireless (T-Mobile) coverage, and regional fiber buildouts (Wire3) — competition is both on coverage and on delivering multi-gig symmetric services.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-d94963c8, EV-56997533, EV-26834797, EV-6e011fda, EV-37a93029

### THEME-3: Mobile bundles and converged offers influence perceived value
- **Description:** Mobile+home-broadband bundling (Spectrum Mobile, AT&T wireless discounts, T-Mobile bundle credits) appears in company messaging and local offering pages; for price/value switchers, bundling reduces effective household spend and is used as a retention/ACV lever.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1, RQ2
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-33618605, EV-3ccb3abd, EV-3a643017, EV-07620654

## 7. SWOT analysis

### Opportunities
- **SWOT-O1:** Marion County has active broadband investment and unserved pockets; regional FTTH rollouts (Wire3) can capture share against legacy operators. [EV-d94963c8, EV-56997533]
- **SWOT-O2:** Bundling home internet with mobile or streaming (promos) remains an effective conversion lever for switchers. [EV-4f63202e, EV-33618605, EV-3a643017]

### Threats
- **SWOT-T1:** Incumbent national providers (Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile) offer aggressive promotions, national scale and bundle advantages that can limit regional growth. [EV-31d73084, EV-91207f04, EV-944ad5f5]
