## 6. Market themes

### THEME-1: Promotional pricing and streaming bundles drive sign-ups
- **Description:** Providers (regional entrants and incumbents) use time-limited promos (first month free, first-year discounts, gift-card rebates) and streaming bundle credits to reduce friction and accelerate sign-ups during rollout windows.
- **Related research questions:** RQ2, RQ3, RQ7
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, Spectrum, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-4f63202e, EV-89785427, EV-951542ea, EV-fe02126f

### THEME-2: Mobile + internet bundles affect perceived price/value
- **Description:** Mobile bundles and cross-product discounts (Spectrum Mobile, AT&T, T-Mobile) change the effective price customers pay and are used by incumbents to increase retention and justify higher post-promo prices.
- **Related research questions:** RQ2, RQ3
- **Competitors involved:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet
- **Evidence:** EV-33618605, EV-bf5f8187, EV-32618472

### THEME-3: Local fiber construction confers availability advantage
- **Description:** Active, visible fiber buildouts (press releases, 'halfway' milestones) let regional ISPs claim availability and offer multi-gig services in target markets, differentiating them from cable/5G incumbents.
- **Related research questions:** RQ2, RQ4
- **Competitors involved:** Wire3, AT&T, Spectrum
- **Evidence:** EV-d94963c8, EV-bab0d975, EV-db5591b3

### THEME-4: Reliability and unlimited data remain decision drivers
- **Description:** Incumbents emphasize reliability and 'no-data-cap' positioning; fixed-wireless plans may exhibit priority thresholds or congestion policies that can push heavy users to fiber.
- **Related research questions:** RQ1

### THEME-5: Local support is a small-business differentiator

- **Sequence order:** 3

- **Activities:**

- **Exit criteria:** Sustained monthly net adds and retention above target thresholds; referral program drives a growing share of new sign-ups.

## 16. Success metrics
