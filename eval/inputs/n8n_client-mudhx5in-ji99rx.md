## 3. Value proposition
- Headline: Local fiber with transparent pricing, symmetric speeds, and responsive hometown support. [THEME-2, THEME-1, SWOT-O1]
- Supporting points:
- Predictable pricing with clear post-promo expectations to avoid bill shock. [THEME-1]
- Symmetric gigabit-class fiber where available, prioritized for newly served neighborhoods. [THEME-2, SWOT-O1]
- Local, U.S.-based technicians and faster issue resolution versus national call centers. [THEME-2]
- No bundle gap response: Offer targeted bill credits or partner discounts for customers switching from bundled national providers and create a simple 'bring-your-phone' credit option to narrow effective monthly cost differences. [brief]
- Low brand recognition response: Invest early in visible local sponsorships, referral bonuses, and targeted address-level direct mail highlighting local install success stories to build trust quickly. [inference]

## 9. Risks

### RISK-1
- Statement: National carriers use bundled mobile+home discounts to retain customers and undercut regional pricing. [THEME-3, SWOT-T1]
- Likelihood: high [THEME-3]
- Impact: high [SWOT-T1]
- Mitigation: Targeted partnership discounts, competitive effective-rate offers, and employer-based deals to narrow bundle gap. [THEME-3]

### RISK-2
- Statement: Customer dissatisfaction from promotional roll-off and perceived hidden costs. [THEME-1, SWOT-W1]
- Likelihood: medium [THEME-1]
- Impact: medium [SWOT-W1]
- Mitigation: Publish clear blended-12mo pricing, provide grace offers at promo end, and emphasize total cost of ownership vs. competitors. [THEME-1]

### RISK-3
- Statement: Delays in buildout or installation logistics slow activation and damage local reputation. [THEME-2, SWOT-O1]
- Likelihood: medium [THEME-2]
- Impact: high [THEME-2]
- Mitigation: Set realistic install windows, over-communicate status to customers, and reserve install crews for prioritized neighborhoods. [THEME-2, ASM-1]
