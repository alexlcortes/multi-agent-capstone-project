## 1. Ideal customer profiles

### ICP-1: Residential price/value switcher (Ocala)
- **Description:** Households in Ocala that shop primarily on initial price and perceived value (promo-driven), but will switch for reliable multi-gig fiber, transparent pricing and local support. [THEME-1, THEME-3, SWOT-W1]
- **Current provider:** Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet [THEME-2, THEME-3]
- **Demographic signals:** 
  - Price-sensitive; responsive to time-limited promos and streaming credits. [THEME-1]
  - Households with multiple streamers and remote workers who value reliability and unlimited data. [THEME-4]

### ICP-2: Small-business price/value switcher (Ocala)
- **Description:** Local small businesses frustrated with incumbents' distant support or variable reliability, open to switching for faster local technician response and predictable billing. [THEME-5, SWOT-O2]
- **Current provider:** Spectrum, AT&T [THEME-3, THEME-5]
- **Demographic signals:** 
  - Values local field technicians and faster on-site SLAs over pure price. [THEME-5, SWOT-O2]
  - Likely to be influenced by professional-install inclusions and uptime guarantees. [EV-fe02126f, EV-91207f04]

## 2. Customer pains and desired outcomes

### PAIN-1
- **Pain statement:** Promo-driven incumbent pricing leads to initial low monthly costs but causes confusion and post-promo bill shock. [THEME-1, SWOT-W2]
- **Desired outcome:** Clear, predictable pricing (transparent post-promo rates and blended examples) and simple, communicated renewal terms. [THEME-1]
- **Related icp ids:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Severity:** high [THEME-1, SWOT-W2]

### PAIN-2
- **Pain statement:** Mobile + internet bundles from incumbents change effective price and make simple Internet-only comparisons difficult. [THEME-2]
- **Desired outcome:** A value narrative that normalizes the lack of a mobile bundle (explicitly explaining comparative total cost of ownership) or offers alternative bundle equivalents. [ASM-1, THEME-2]
- **Related icp ids:** ICP-1
- **Severity:** medium [THEME-2, ASM-1]

### PAIN-3
- **Pain statement:** Low local brand recognition for Wire3 reduces trust among switchers compared with national incumbents. [SWOT-W1]
- **Desired outcome:** Fast awareness and social proof (local PR, testimonials, visible construction milestones) to build trust and lower perceived risk of switching. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1]
- **Related icp ids:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Severity:** medium [SWOT-W1, THEME-3]

## 3. Value proposition
- **Headline:** Ocala’s local fiber alternative: predictable pricing, multi-gig reliability, and fast local support. [THEME-3, THEME-1, THEME-5]
- **Supporting points:** 
  - Multi-gig symmetric fiber plans with included eero Wi‑Fi and professional installation. [SWOT-S1, EV-33d161e7, EV-d1118463]
  - Promotions to lower early friction (first month free, streaming credits) timed with build milestones. [THEME-1, SWOT-O1]
  - Local technicians and faster on-site response for residential and small-business customers. [THEME-5, SWOT-O2]
- **No bundle gap response:** Explicitly acknowledge Wire3 does not offer a mobile bundle; position Internet + streaming + included Wi‑Fi devices as an alternative value stack and offer partner streaming credits/referral discounts to narrow the perceived gap. [ASM-1, SWOT-O1]
- **Low brand recognition response:** Accelerate visible local credibility via construction milestone PR, neighborhood install spotlights, local testimonials, and trial promotions tied to on-site pro installs. [THEME-3, SWOT-W1]

## 4. Positioning and differentiation
- **Positioning statement:** Wire3 is Ocala’s locally built fiber provider offering multi-gig symmetric speeds, transparent launch pricing, and rapid local support—an alternative to incumbent bundle-driven offers. [THEME-3, THEME-1, SWOT-S1, SWOT-W1]
- **Differentiators:** 
  - Visible local fiber construction and availability claims backed by region-specific rollout messaging. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1]
  - Included enhanced Wi‑Fi (eero) and professional installation to reduce friction at sign-up. [EV-33d161e7, EV-fe02126f]
  - Local field technicians and small-business support emphasis for faster SLAs. [THEME-5, SWOT-O2]
- **Competitive frame:** Wire3, Spectrum, AT&T, T-Mobile Home Internet

- **Supporting message:** Lead with promo details and clearly communicate post-promo pricing examples to reduce bill-shock and build trust. [THEME-1, SWOT-W2]

## 7. Launch phases and activities

### PHASE-1: Awareness & availability
- **Sequence order:** 1
- **Timeframe:** Weeks 0-6 (pre- and early-rollout)
- **Activities:** 
  - Publish availability map, construction milestone PR and neighborhood-level landing pages with promo messaging. [THEME-3, SWOT-S1]
  - Run targeted digital ads to price/value switchers promoting first-month-free and streaming credits. [THEME-1]
- **Exit criteria:** Availability pages live for target neighborhoods; initial digital campaigns achieving target CTR and landing-page conversion rates (see metrics).

### PHASE-2: Convert & onboard
- **Sequence order:** 2
- **Timeframe:** Weeks 4-16 (peak install window)
- **Activities:** 
  - Offer clear promo pricing with explicit post-promo examples at checkout; enable pro-install scheduling and local tech introductions. [THEME-1, EV-fe02126f]
  - Activate referral incentives and streaming partner credits at install to offset lack of mobile bundle. [ASM-1, SWOT-O1]
- **Exit criteria:** Achieve target new-customer sign-ups in build areas and maintain installation quality KPIs below defect threshold.

### PHASE-3: Retain & scale
- **Sequence order:** 3
- **Timeframe:** Months 3-12 (post-rollout scale)
- **Activities:** 
  - Push referral campaigns, monitor local NPS and quickly remediate installation complaints with local crews. [THEME-5, SWOT-T2]
  - Iterate pricing packages and consider limited third-party mobile/ISP partnerships if needed to close bundle-sensitive segments. [THEME-2, ASM-1]
- **Exit criteria:** Sustained monthly net adds and retention above target thresholds; referral program drives a growing share of new sign-ups.

## 8. Success metrics

### METRIC-1: Net new subscribers in Ocala (first 6 months)
- **Definition:** Count of activated residential and small-business subscriptions in target Ocala neighborhoods within 6 months of campaign start.
- **Target value:** 1,000 net new subscribers in 6 months (illustrative regional target) [THEME-3, THEME-1]
- **Measurement cadence:** monthly

### METRIC-2: Promo-to-paid conversion rate (month 1 -> month 3)
- **Definition:** Share of promo-originated customers who remain active after the immediate promo period (indicator of post-promo satisfaction).
- **Target value:** >= 70% retained past initial promo period (inference) [THEME-1, SWOT-W2]
- **Measurement cadence:** monthly

### METRIC-3: Installation quality / defect rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of installs requiring rework or causing customer complaints within 30 days.
- **Target value:** <= 3% rework/complaint rate (inference) [SWOT-T2, THEME-5]
- **Measurement cadence:** weekly

### METRIC-4: Local NPS (residential & small business)
- **Definition:** Net Promoter Score measured for Ocala customers to assess local credibility and support performance.
- **Target value:** NPS >= 40 within 6 months (inference) [THEME-5, SWOT-W1]
- **Measurement cadence:** monthly

## 9. Risks

### RISK-1
- **Statement:** Incumbents use aggressive mobile+internet bundles and promo stacking to defend customers, making switch economics harder for Wire3. [SWOT-T1, THEME-2]
- **Likelihood:** high [SWOT-T1]
- **Impact:** high [THEME-2, SWOT-T1]
- **Mitigation:** Emphasize alternative value (streaming credits, included Wi‑Fi), transparent TCO comparisons, and local support benefits; explore partnerships to mimic bundle economics. [SWOT-O1, ASM-1, THEME-1]

### RISK-2
- **Statement:** Local reports of installation damage or service disruptions could slow adoption during rollout. [SWOT-T2]
- **Likelihood:** medium [SWOT-T2]
- **Impact:** high [SWOT-T2]
- **Mitigation:** Deploy quality-grade installation SOPs, rapid local remediation teams, and proactive communications; surface testimonials from clean installs promptly. [THEME-5, SWOT-O2]

### RISK-3
- **Statement:** Post-promo churn or perceived bill-shock if post-promo rates are unclear or high. [THEME-1, SWOT-W2]
- **Likelihood:** high [THEME-1]
- **Impact:** medium [SWOT-W2]
- **Mitigation:** Publish clear post-promo pricing examples at point-of-sale, offer blended 12-month pricing options and proactive renewal reminders to set expectations. [THEME-1]
